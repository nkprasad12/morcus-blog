## Dictionary

### Partial Forcellini Support

The [Forcellini Latin dictionary](http://lexica.linguax.com/) (_Lexicon Totius Latinitatis_) is now
indexed on Morcus. When you look up a Latin word, Forcellini entries will appear in the search results.

Each entry links to the full text on Lexica Linguax, with an option to open it in a new tab. Support for inflected terms is still incomplete.

### Corpus Search: 3 Million Words

The corpus search database has grown to about 3 million words — roughly half of the classical
corpus, up from the previous ~2 million (~⅓ of the corpus).
