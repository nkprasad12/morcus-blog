## Texts

The `Library` page is now one of three tabs in a new `Texts` section:

- [Library](https://morcus.net/library) has curated works. These have been checked to make sure the formatting displays correctly and that passages have stable, linkable citations.
- [Experimental](https://morcus.net/experimentalTexts) has a much larger set of works that haven't been through those checks.
- [Custom](https://morcus.net/externalReader) is for reading texts not (yet) available in the other tabs.

### Library

The curated library has more than doubled in size, growing from about 125 works (~3 million words) to 318 works (~6.6 million words). Almost the entire Latin collection from the Perseus Digital Library is now available, along with several works from The Latin Library. Highlights include all of Plautus and Terence, Seneca's tragedies and letters, Cicero's letters, and Roman law texts.

### Experimental

The new Experimental tab offers over 1,600 additional works from about 500 authors, drawn from the _Patrologia Latina_ and _The Latin Library_, and filterable by source and historical period (_Ancient_, _Medieval_, and _Neo-Latin_).

Because these texts haven't been individually curated, they are more likely to have typos or formatting quirks than works in the library, but they open in the same reader with click-to-lookup dictionary support.

### Custom

When importing a web page, site navigation, headers, footers, and sidebars are now stripped out automatically.

## Dictionary

### Saved Dictionary Sets

In the dictionary settings, you can now save the dictionaries you have selected under a name, and switch between sets in a click (for example, to move between Latin–English and Latin–Latin dictionaries).

### Colored Text

Under _Color coding_ in the dictionary settings, you can now choose to color the text of grammatical terms instead of highlighting their background.

## Appearance

The _Appearance_ menu now includes a `Contrast` setting (_Softer_, _Normal_, _Higher_, _Highest_), saved separately for light and dark themes.
