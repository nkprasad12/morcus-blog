## Dictionary

### English and Number links in articles

Clicking on a word in a Latin to English dictionary now brings up results in
English to Latin dictionaries when possible. Note that inflections in English are not yet handled.

### Highlighting for gender

In Lewis and Short, terms related to gender (masculine, feminine, neuter, and abbreviations of these) now show up with the same highlighting as other grammatical
terms (such as case) so that it is easier to scan for these terms in large articles.

### Typo fixes in Smith and Hall

Many user-reported errors were fixed in Smith and Hall - thank you!

## Library

### Faster Resizing

Resizing panels is now much more performant (this fixes an issue of freezing when there was a lot of text on the screen).

### Latin Texts

The following are new!

- `Naturales Quastiones` by `Seneca`
- `De Fato` by `Cicero`
- `Ab Urbe Condita` by `Livy`

### Translations

Translations are now available for:

- `De Amicitia` by `Cicero`
- `Ab Urbe Condita` by `Livy`
